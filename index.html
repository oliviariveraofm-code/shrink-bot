01 · The Philosophy

No consensus, no call.

Every agent reads the chart from one angle — structure, liquidity, timing, macro, order flow, psychology. They debate in silence. If they don’t agree, there is no setup. Period.

Trade Card

Consensus reached. One card with exact entry, SL, TP1, TP2, size and expiry.

No Trade

No consensus. That’s an outcome too — with the exact level to watch and the trigger that would still produce a card. Not trading is a position.

Confidence = honest confluence count. Minimum 65% to trade. Capped at 90%. Never inflated.

09 · Hard Rules

Hard rules.

R01
NO ENTRIES during Judas window 09:00–09:30 on GBPUSD, EURUSD, GBPJPY. Fakeout probability too high.
R02
DEAD ZONE after 20:00 Dubai. Zero entries. System hard-stops. Asia session = no edge.
R03
NO TRADE if high-impact news within 60 minutes of entry. Prop firm: override permanently blocked.
R04
R:R minimum 1.5. Below that = automatic veto by HEDGE. Card will not be issued.
R05
No entry mid-range. Requires a clean POI (OB or FVG). Mid-range entries are the fastest way to blow stops.
R06
After 2 consecutive losses → size drops 50% automatically. Flag: REDUCED SIZING ⚠️
R07
Drawdown 3–5% → max 0.5% risk per trade. Drawdown >5% → NO TRADE until drawdown: reset issued.
R08
CRYPTO = OUT OF SCOPE. No card issued. Response: ⛔ OUT OF DNA.
R09
Every card expires. Strategy A: +45min. Strategy B: +30min. NY prime: +90min. NY cont: +60min. Expired card = NO TRADE.
R10
AT TP1 — always close 50% of position and move SL to break-even. Non-negotiable. Protect the trade.
